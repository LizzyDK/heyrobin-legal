Hey Robin — Privacy Policy

Nuthatch LLC · Last updated: July 19, 2026

Hey Robin is a travel-planning app made by Nuthatch LLC ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices. Short version: your data is used only to run the app's features — never sold, never used for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (for sign-in and sync).
  • Trip content: trips, itineraries, bookings you enter, budgets, gift lists, chats with Robin, and photos you attach to trips.
  • Location (only while using the app, and only when you use guide mode or "What should I do today?"): your approximate position, used to plan and guide around where you are.
  • Photos you choose to snap for Robin (dishes, menus, landmarks), used to generate an answer about them.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and file storage. Your account and trip data live there.
  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI behind Robin. When you use AI features, relevant trip details, chats, and — for guide features — your location and photos you snap are sent to Anthropic to generate the response. The app asks for your explicit consent before the first AI use.

Both act as processors on our behalf. We use no advertising or tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics.

Retention & deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Sharing between users

If you share a trip, the people you invite can see that trip's content and your display name. You control invitations, and members can be removed at any time by the trip owner.

Your choices

  • Location and camera access are optional and controlled in iOS Settings; core planning works without them.
  • You can decline the AI-data consent; AI features stay off until you agree.
  • Contact us to exercise applicable data rights (access, correction, deletion).
